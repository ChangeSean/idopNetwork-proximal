# Independent discovery and estimation protocol

Frozen before outcomes of this study are computed. Existing studies remain separate.

1. Each generated dataset or eligible clinical cohort receives one random 50:50 split. No repeat split or selection using clinical outcomes is permitted. Simulation dataset offset 120000; partition seed 20261200 + 10000 * scenario + replicate. Clinical partition seed 20261201, applied to patient identifiers in sorted order.
2. Molecular panel selection, imputation, standardisation, niche curves, eight network candidates, proxy roles, information dimension, readout matrix P and nuisance moment pivots use discovery patients only. Discovery outcomes and follow-up times are not supplied to the design functions. Clinical sample inclusion requires observed event indicator and follow-up time; this defines the cohort before the split. Discovery covariate imputations are applied to estimation patients.
3. Analyse estimation patients without reselecting the design or retesting its rank. All remaining concentrated moments enter the Bonferroni Fieller intersection. Covariance uses 100 estimation-patient resamples in simulation and 300 in clinical application; censoring is refitted in those patients and resamples. P, roles, dimension and pivots remain fixed. One discovery SD is the clinical exposure intervention unit. Simulation uses the raw generating exposure unit.
4. Discovery failure, numerical nuisance failure or <97.5% complete moment resamples returns the whole real line, with a recorded reason and no point estimate. This gives a confidence set for every attempted dataset and every clinical panel exposure. All returned shapes, including empty and disconnected sets, enter coverage; point accuracy uses its explicit point-fit denominator. This completion convention does not correct invalid proxy roles or incomplete readout dimension.
5. Seven existing generating systems each use 200 fresh datasets, two targets, and the same estimation patients and resampling indices for paired Fieller and normal calculations. Report design and point availability, correct dimension and generating-DAG role validity, bias, RMSE, Monte Carlo uncertainty, unconditional confidence-set coverage, coverage among available point fits, normal reference coverage and every set shape. Independent sampling addresses outcome-driven design reuse; valid proximal roles and complete readout representation remain identification conditions.
6. Clinical analysis uses OV and LUAD independently. Report all 60 discovery-selected exposures, discovery and estimation sample sizes and events, role sets and rank, point estimates, confidence sets, and construction or inference status. The pathway boundary file is applied as a separate prespecified mechanistic analysis; its numerical results do not validate biological exclusion conditions.
7. Theorems concern selected models with valid proxy exclusions, a full mean bridge after the discovery projection, and regular nuisance moments. Conditional on discovery, moment inversion covers the same population effect even if effect information is weak. A discovery-validity event with probability 1-o(1) gives unconditional asymptotic coverage. Discovery fluctuations are represented by the distribution of chosen designs across datasets, rather than graph reselection inside estimation resamples.
